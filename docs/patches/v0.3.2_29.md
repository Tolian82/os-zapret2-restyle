# v0.3.2_29 — Stale Strategy Lab worker recovery

## Purpose

Ensure a queued, running, or cancellation-requested job cannot remain nonterminal after its worker process disappears.

## Changes

- validate the worker PID against both process liveness and Strategy Lab command identity;
- reconcile nonterminal jobs whose worker PID is missing, dead, stale, or belongs to another process;
- remove reserved candidate runtime and IPFW residue before restoration;
- restore the recorded initial Zapret2 service state through the normal lifecycle transaction;
- persist terminal `ERROR` when cleanup and restoration are proven, otherwise persist `RESTORE_FAILED`;
- clear stale worker PID and active-job markers;
- run reconciliation from start, status, cancel, and result operations under the launcher lock;
- add the focused stale-worker recovery test to mandatory CI.

## Verification

`test-strategy-lab-stale-worker-recovery.sh` creates a nonterminal job with a dead PID and verifies cleanup, stopped-service restoration, terminal state, and marker removal.
