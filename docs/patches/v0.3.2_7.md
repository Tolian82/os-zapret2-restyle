# v0.3.2_7 — Strategy Lab isolated candidate runtime

## Scope

Patch 5 implements the approved executor for exactly one temporary Zapret2 candidate.
It does not introduce the strategy-family catalog, family screening, parameter
expansion, stability scoring, extended protocols, circular validation, or the final GUI
switch-over.

## Runtime contract

- the normal Zapret2 service is already stopped under the shared lifecycle lock;
- one job-specific runtime directory is created;
- one temporary dvtws2 process is launched without supervisor restart behavior;
- lifecycle-lock descriptor 9 is closed before the child process is created;
- the candidate uses divert port 9989 and the reserved IPFW range 19100–19131;
- temporary rules are limited to TCP/443 and the resolved IPv4 addresses of required
  endpoints;
- up to two required endpoints use the same active candidate concurrently;
- every candidate exit path stops dvtws2 and removes the reserved rules;
- stage 90 repeats cleanup before restoring the exact original Zapret2 state.

The smoke fixture uses Zapret2 syntax only:

`--lua-desync=multisplit:pos=1`

Its result verifies the executor and is not yet a family acceptance decision.

## State and output

The job stores a structured `candidate_smoke` result containing the exact strategy,
endpoint results, and `all_pass`. Stage 50 reports successful executor completion even
when the smoke candidate itself does not open every endpoint; internal runtime,
firewall, launch, probe-collection, or teardown failure remains `ERROR`.

## Automated verification

Focused mocks verify:

- one temporary dvtws2 process;
- target-scoped candidate rules;
- two endpoints under one strategy;
- structured candidate result;
- teardown after success;
- teardown after injected firewall failure;
- no candidate PID, process, or IPFW rule remains.
