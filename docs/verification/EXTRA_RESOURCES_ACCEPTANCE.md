## Execution rules

Owner closure is distinct from technical PASS: **CLOSED BY OWNER (UNVERIFIED)** stops further tests but never implies their execution. An explicitly withheld release is **ON HOLD**. A test becomes **PASS** only with a recorded exact candidate, environment, executed procedure, observed result and evidence source. Active mandatory test states: **NOT RUN**, **IN PROGRESS**, **PASS**, **FAIL**, **BLOCKED**. **CANCELLED BY OWNER** is a separate, explicit historical disposition for rows *removed from scope*, never a substitute for marking a still-mandatory test PASS or silently waiving its evidence. A blocking FAIL requires the smallest reproducible scoped repair, a new test candidate when packaged source changes, and re-execution of impacted tests. Do not silently waive a mandatory row or spin out speculative hardening. Move to the next milestone when the gate is satisfied; propose useful new work with evidence, but acquire owner approval before enlarging the mandatory scope.
