- **Bounded exceptions:** static guard scans `scripts/native-opnsense-package.py`, `scripts/build-pkg.sh`, `scripts/compatibility-bootstrap.sh`, `Makefile` only. Immutable Stage 0 archives, pinned CI guests and unmigrated **Stage 3** runtime Python paths are not covered or claimed migrated. Policy `exceptions: []` authorizes no extra platforms.
- **STOP decision:** duplicate *active native package* ABI table, unsupported admission and circular early Python bootstrap are rejected by the scope's validations. **Stage 1 source/native-CI scope accepted**, subject to _70 exact-head PR/merge; *not* Stage 2–4 full lifecycle safety.
