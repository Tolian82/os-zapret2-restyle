## Gap and actual change

The _114 signed receipt and signed hour-bounded policy have exact file SHA, RSA verification, expiry and deny lists but **do not consume an approval**. Even a non-expired and not-revoked receipt can be reused; `sequence` was only a positive integer, not a verified local high-watermark. There is also no owner-authenticated latest snapshot, trusted clock or out-of-band rollback witness.

_115 adds a **separate CI-only isolated local ledger primitive** (`scripts/stage3-prepkg-approval-ledger.py`). This tool is **NOT** wired into the privileged updater, existing signed `-c` read-only execution route, OPNsense Web/firmware/managed CLI, native hooks, configd or the real package. Its job is to exercise precisely the file-atomic, fail-closed component of a future safe update admission before taking any risk with `pkg`.

The three allowed commands are strictly `initialize`, `consume` and `inspect`, and **all require** actual root FreeBSD amd64 **and** `GITHUB_ACTIONS=true`, `ZAPRET2_STAGE0_DISPOSABLE_OFFICIAL_MK=1`, `ZAPRET2_STAGE3_PREPKG_ARCHIVE_BOOTSTRAP=1`, `ZAPRET2_STAGE3_DISPOSABLE_LEDGER=1`. All paths must be canonical under root-owned private `/root` with final directory `approval-ledger` mode `0700`, never installed OPNsense paths. The module contains **no subprocess or pkg dispatch** and does not create or manage a production key.

Each local `consume` receives explicit positive sequence plus test-only authority SHA, policy SHA and receipt SHA **from its caller**; the module is deliberately *not* an independent signature/issuer verifier. The genuine disposable OPNsense harness first executes the complete real signed _114 trust-seed / RSA claims / two native archive hashes / original FD9 cold read-only chain. **Only after PASS** the harness uses those test-only values with a local isolated ledger. The second synthetic sequence is only a filesystem transaction fixture, **not** a second approved and signed release.

### Local atomicity contract

- Exclusive nonblocking `fcntl.flock(LOCK_EX|LOCK_NB)` on a root-owned, single-linked, zero-byte `0600` `ledger.lock` under a pinned `0700` directory FD; parallel attempts fail closed (`LEDGER_BUSY_FAIL_CLOSED`) or see the spent record and fail replay checks.
- While holding the lock, scan the entire bounded `spent-<12digit-sequence>-<receipt-sha>.json` journal using anchored directory-relative `openat`, `O_NOFOLLOW`, one-link root `0600` and stable descriptor/inode/size/mtime/ctime checks. Strict JSON keys/no duplicates; SHA digest formats; unique receipt and policy SHA; sequence must be **contiguous from 1** and each record's `previous_entry_sha256` must link to SHA256 of the previous complete record.
- Only the **next exact sequence** may be spent, with a receipt SHA and policy SHA that have never been spent. Create its final journal filename using atomic `O_CREAT|O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW`, write exact canonical bytes, `fsync` record, then `fsync` directory. No temporary rename/replacement or automatic deletion of partial writes.
- If power/process interruption happens just after exclusive file creation, a zero/truncated/malformed entry poisons future `inspect` and `consume`: **refuse all further admissions**, never silently overwrite, auto-delete, retry or permit a different transaction through. Recovery would require a separate explicit offline owner-approved investigation; no unsafe repair action is offered.
- Corrupt/mutated entries, unknown files, forged basenames, symlinks, modes, hardlinks, invalid chains and sequence rollback/gaps must refuse. Max **512 records**; no unbounded write growth.

**This is not externally tamperproof.** An attacker with root can copy/rewrite an entire self-consistent local ledger or delete its last entry and roll back the observed high-watermark; a local SHA chain cannot detect removal of the final entry without an **independent anchored high-watermark / external witness**. OS/filesystem crash guarantees are no stronger than the actual `fsync` durability delivered by the storage stack; crash before persistence cannot be equated with a committed receipt. There is also **no trusted authority/policy/current-issue proof** in this module. Its `CI_LOCAL_ATOMIC_SPENT_NOT_PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY` status must never be interpreted as authorization to update.
