## Warm-readiness result

The `_12` corrective removed the deterministic one-second polling granularity identified in `_11` while retaining two consecutive qualifying readiness snapshots and the existing readiness predicates.

Across all 46 physical profile-segment startups recorded by the five replays:

- median `pool_startup_ms`: `82.5 ms`;
- mean: `93.87 ms`;
- p90: `163 ms`;
- min/max: `80 / 171 ms`.

The higher approximately `163-171 ms` samples are logical batches containing two sequential profile-compatible Model-C segments; normal one-segment batches are consistently around `80-90 ms`.

This replaces the `_11` pattern of approximately `1056-1066 ms` for one physical profile segment and approximately `2116-2126 ms` for two sequential segments. The deterministic readiness-polling defect is therefore confirmed fixed on the owner appliance.

## Cross-batch reuse decision

The original `_7` decision boundary says to close cross-batch lifecycle reuse when the conservative amortizable upper bound is not material relative to observed Stage-60 jitter.

After `_12`, the median theoretical upper bound is only `3260 ms` while observed Stage-60 stdev is `2370.888 ms` and the five-run min/max spread is `5600 ms`. Because `3260 ms` is only an upper bound and actual reuse cannot eliminate all of it, the remaining achievable gain is not cleanly separable from current live run-to-run variation and does not justify the additional stateful cross-batch worker architecture.

Decision:

- accept `_12` warm-readiness polling corrective;
- retain current per-batch Model-C lifecycle;
- do **not** implement cross-batch keep-warm/reuse;
- do **not** change Model-C candidate width, adaptive planner, source-port dispatch, discovery GET-4K, Model-B fallback or cold Model-A containment;
- close the lifecycle reuse optimization question as **NO FURTHER PRODUCTION CHANGE**.
