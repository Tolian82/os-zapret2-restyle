## Speed interpretation

The accepted Model A cold-reference total-candidate median is `1580 ms`. Comparing that
cold per-candidate median with the mean already-warm dispatch+probe value of `212.7 ms`
shows about an `86.5%` reduction in the steady candidate path. That is a useful mechanism
signal, not a full-search result.

If the measured three-worker pool startup is amortized over three candidate probes, the
narrow retained-metric estimate is approximately:

`(1163.6 + 3 * 212.7) / 3 = 600.6 ms/candidate`.

Against the Model A `1580 ms` cold median, that corresponds to roughly a `62.0%` reduction.
This is the more conservative percentage for the already executed three-worker experiment.

Neither percentage is a worst-case Strategy Lab wall-clock result. The coexistence harness
contains only a small selected corpus and also performs deliberate stop/death/restoration
checks. A real successful Strategy Lab job can stop Stage 60 early when enough candidates
work, while a no-candidate target such as `telegram.org` exhausts the available graph and is
the correct workload for worst-case search timing.

## Consequence for the next experiment

The next measurement must replay an exact `NO_CANDIDATE / graph_exhausted` Stage-60 corpus
and preserve its original candidate order. The benchmark must report complete-corpus warm
wall time, per-batch startup/cleanup, dispatch/probe timing, RSS, exact search-result
equivalence, deterministic route attribution and final semantic restoration. Model B
remains experiment-only and `production_approved=false` until that evidence exists and an
explicit architecture decision is made.
