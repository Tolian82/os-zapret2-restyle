## Startup/readiness result

Stable-readiness summary:

| Variant | min ms | median ms | p90 ms | max ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BLOB-free | 62.250 | 63.061 | 65.124 | 65.124 |
| built-in | 61.380 | 62.652 | 69.456 | 69.456 |
| external | 62.087 | 62.566 | 69.626 | 69.626 |

Pairwise median differences:

- built-in vs BLOB-free: `-0.409 ms` / `-0.649%`;
- external vs BLOB-free: `-0.495 ms` / `-0.785%`;
- external vs built-in: `-0.086 ms` / `-0.137%`.

The BLOB-bearing variants were not slower at the median. The absolute differences are below
1% and are smaller than the observed within-variant spread/tails, so this run does not show a
material startup/readiness penalty from either the built-in fake or the representative
external `226`-byte BLOB.

## Decision

Accept `_3` owner-live measurement for its defined three-variant scope.

Do **not** change production Model C BLOB loading. The representative external-file case shows
no material readiness or RSS cost, and the architecture contract explicitly forbids using one
valid run as production-change authorization.

This does not claim that arbitrary BLOB count/size has zero cost. The broader experiment plan
still calls for small-inline and several semantically compatible external-resource coverage.
If the project continues the BLOB-loading optimization investigation, that scaling/common-set
question is the next measurement boundary rather than a production rewrite.
