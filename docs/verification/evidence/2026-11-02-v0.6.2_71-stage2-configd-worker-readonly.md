This increment adds **no signalling or package-hook changes**. `scripts/stage2-configd-worker-readonly.py` checks PID-file trust, watcher command, direct-worker parent/command, start-time identity, `configctl system status`, then repeats the complete snapshot; it refuses missing/multiple processes, unexpected parent/PID, changed birth time and unavailable status. The output explicitly sets `signals_sent=false`, `restart_authorized=false`, `pid_reuse_excluded_after_observation=false`. Read-only snapshots are **not** authorization to send SIGTERM later (TOCTOU).
