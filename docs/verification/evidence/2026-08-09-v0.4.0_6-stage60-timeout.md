## Diagnosis

`0.4.0_6` closes the observed Stage-50 parent-timeout defect for this live target, but it
exposes the next timeout-hierarchy defect in Stage 60:

1. Stage 60 had a fixed 70-second enclosing limit although the job still had search budget.
2. The expansion loop admitted another cold candidate without first proving that its full
   execution/termination/cleanup lifecycle fit inside the remaining parent window.
3. The enclosing timeout therefore terminated Stage 60 in the middle of the thirteenth
   candidate instead of allowing the search policy to stop at a safe boundary.
4. The partial `parameter-expansion.json` consequently retained an empty
   `stopped_reason` rather than a truthful budget-exhaustion classification.

Corrective source candidate: `v0.4.0_7`.
