## Interpretation and next gate

This is **a measurement of original child-Status behavior under an inherited outer FD9**, not a proposal to ship an external lock wrapper. A completed cold Status is not proof of atomic pre-pkg admission; a bounded noncompletion is not proof of a specific lockf syscall. Neither result protects the independent OPNsense firmware/Web/CLI/root entrypoints, guarantees Config/process/IPFW continuity or fixes the confirmed native hook-veto bypass.

**LH-08/AC-11 remain BLOCKED.** The next design gate remains an actually enforceable supported pre-pkg entrypoint and continuous owner-aware serialization through real native pkg, with a nonrecursive hook handoff and no-mutation unsafe-owner refusal. Keep historical original package and published assets immutable, and do not rewrite the four production hooks before that boundary is proven.
