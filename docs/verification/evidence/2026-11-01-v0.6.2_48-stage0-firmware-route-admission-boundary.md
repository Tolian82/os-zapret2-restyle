Official 26.7 source: [opnsense/core pinned](https://github.com/opnsense/core/tree/874c32d7e5e337f19f256066b9689e91e56d5277). Previous [native actual pkg root experiment](2026-11-01-v0.6.2_47-native-root-pkg-and-owner-negative.md) confirmed explicit privileged pkg unlock → delete of a synthetic fixture.

Product support of a managed update path requires an independently implemented atomic/serialized old-owner/FD9/process/foreign-IPFW pretransaction admission and exact native successor rollback tests, not just this classifier. An uncoordinated privileged root may override local policy; no package claims to veto all such actions. This contract does not disable OPNsense firmware or modify global firmware source.
