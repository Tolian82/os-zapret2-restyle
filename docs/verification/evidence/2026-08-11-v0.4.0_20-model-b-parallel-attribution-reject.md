## Root cause identified after the run

`_20` made route attribution depend on curl write-out reporting both the connected
`remote_ip` and the connected `local_port` for every endpoint probe. That is valid for a
successful connection, but it is too strict for a blocked/failed probe: curl may never
establish a socket and therefore may have no connected remote/local values to report.

The IPFW rule already matches the exact controlled TCP source port, exact pinned destination
IPv4, destination port 443, local-origin traffic and WAN interface. Counter growth on that
rule therefore proves that traffic matching the controlled selector reached the dedicated
worker path. `_21` keeps connected `remote_ip` and `local_port` mandatory for PASS, while
failed-probe route attribution instead requires exact executed-command binding (`--local-port`
and `--resolve`) plus exact-rule counter growth and successful rule cleanup.

Production Strategy Lab remains Model A and `production_approved=false` remains unchanged.
