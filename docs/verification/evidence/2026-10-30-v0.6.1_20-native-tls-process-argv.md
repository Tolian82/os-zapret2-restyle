## Initial failures recorded without reinterpretation

Native [CI #41124279664](https://github.com/Tolian82/os-zapret2-restyle/actions/runs/41124279664) failed at test fixture startup (private temporary Lua directory was inaccessible after native privilege drop); this was corrected by permitting read-only directory traversal, matching the previously proven isolated `fake` harness. [CI #41124539122](https://github.com/Tolian82/os-zapret2-restyle/actions/runs/41124539122) and [CI #41125287883](https://github.com/Tolian82/os-zapret2-restyle/actions/runs/41125287883) exposed that `sysctl -b kern.proc.args.<PID>` returns an unknown OID on FreeBSD 15.1. The final test resolves the native `kern.proc.args` MIB through libc `sysctlnametomib()` and appends the PID as a **numeric** component of `sysctl(3)`, instead of relying on a dynamic text OID. The acceptance was **not** weakened.
