Therefore the Strategy Lab worker was denied before it could create a lifecycle snapshot, and subsequent stale recovery could not establish restoration. This is **not** evidence of curl-impersonate failure. A separate early PID race cannot be asserted from missing post-reconciliation PID, but is not needed to explain this observed error.
