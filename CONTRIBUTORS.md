# Contributors

[os-zapret2-restyle](https://github.com/Tolian82/os-zapret2-restyle) is an
independent OPNsense plugin maintained by [Tolian82](https://github.com/Tolian82).
Thanks to the people and projects whose work makes it possible.

## Project maintenance and development

- [Tolian82](https://github.com/Tolian82) — os-zapret2-restyle project
  maintenance, implementation, OPNsense integration and documentation.

## Upstream projects and acknowledgements

- [bol-van/zapret2](https://github.com/bol-van/zapret2) — Zapret2 DPI-bypass
  engine, Lua runtime and strategy mechanisms used by the plugin.
- [ugorur/os-zapret2](https://github.com/ugorur/os-zapret2) — earlier OPNsense
  integration and project lineage, maintained by Umur Gorur.
- **[lexiforest/curl-impersonate](https://github.com/lexiforest/curl-impersonate)**
  — upstream browser-impersonating HTTP/TLS/QUIC client used by Strategy Lab
  for Chrome-compatible connectivity checks and HTTP/3 support.
- [OPNsense](https://opnsense.org/) — firewall platform and plugin framework.

The upstream projects are credited as dependencies or sources of prior work;
listing them does **not** mean their maintainers have directly contributed
commits to os-zapret2-restyle or endorse this project.
